About

I’m an open source leader with 7+ years building and scaling enterprise open source governance across VMware, Broadcom, and Omnissa — from a portfolio of 10,000+ engineers and hundreds of products to a net-new function built single-handedly.

I led open source governance through one of the largest transitions in the software industry: the Broadcom acquisition of VMware and the subsequent carve-out into Omnissa — ensuring continuity of compliance and community engagement throughout. My work spans executive leadership, legal, engineering, security, and product, turning open source from a risk-management function into a strategic business capability.
